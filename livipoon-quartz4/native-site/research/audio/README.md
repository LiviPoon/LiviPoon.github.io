# Research story audio

Place the final audio track in this folder using this exact filename:

`story.m4a`

The research experience maps the complete audio duration to the complete horizontal story:

- `0:00` displays the opening Listen scene.
- The middle of the recording displays the middle of the story ribbon.
- The end of the recording displays the final scene.
- Scrolling manually seeks the recording to the corresponding location.

The page expects the deployed file at `/research/audio/story.m4a`. Keep the
container and extension consistent: the animation uses the duration reported
by the browser to map the recording precisely across the horizontal story.

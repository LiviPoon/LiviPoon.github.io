Let's face it, AI is here to stay for the foreseeable future. It is up to us to decide whether or not it will drastically affect our ability to have autonomy and free will.

One unforeseen benefit of having freewill and autonomy is the guide rails we put on our own day-to-day usage. To my dismay many of our peers do not think consciously about this -- in part to the pitfalls of social media.

Surrounded by a pessimism we can't seem to shake, I was surprised to learn that there are a lot of institutions and companies who are taking the effort to understand how these systems can and should be used. From AI security boards to the development of safe AI usage, we are at a point where people are questioning how and when AI should be used. What an exciting time! For there is hope, and where there is hope, there is life.

Whether or not you like the idea of it, every field that will have some use case for AI -- the creative sector being one of the least touched (hopefully). 

Here are some guide rails I have assimilated as of late:

1) **Utilize AI Incrementally** -- *Do not ask it to complete entire tasks, rather ask it to complete small tasks and review the small tasks it completes.*

2) **Never Use it To Plan Goals** -- *Once you take planning autonomy from yourself, you risk putting too much into the hands of the AI. Once that happens it is hard to justify or explain decisions it makes.*

3) **Do it Yourself!** -- *Take a moment to ask, "Would it be beneficial to just write the thing myself"?*

4) **Never Let it Give You Answers** -- *Ask it to help you with your homework rather than letting it give you the answer! (With the understanding that it too can make mistakes.)*

5) **Who Do I Want to Become?** -- *Every time you use AI you are setting yourself up to be closer to the engineer operating on Tesla cars and every time you don't you are setting yourself up to be closer to the engineer operating a car from the 1980's. Who do you want to become?*

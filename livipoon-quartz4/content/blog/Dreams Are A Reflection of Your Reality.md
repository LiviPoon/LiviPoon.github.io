I vivid dream almost every night and I think I will be sharing more of them here -- some of them are realistic and involve people I know in real life. When this occurs they are often true. 

For example, one night during high school I had a very sad dream about someone who I knew and cared deeply about. I think it reminded me that we all are struggling with something and sometimes we don't realize that those close to us need a shoulder to cry own.

*The hallway was dim, lit only by a few tired fluorescent lights. The third floor corridors at school were always narrow and quiet at night. My footsteps muffled against the olive green carpet as I walked.*

*Then I heard it. At first it was faint, but as I moved forward it became clearer. The sound came in soft, uneven breaths that broke into quiet sobs. Something about it felt familiar and yet so distant.*

*As I turned the corner I saw her sitting on the floor against the wall. Her knees were pulled close to her chest and her face was buried in her hands. Her shoulders trembled with each breath, and strands of her hair stuck to her damp cheeks.*

*I slowed down as I reached her and lowered myself to the floor. For a while we simply sat there in the quiet hallway while her crying filled the space around us.

*Air hung still, as if even the dust motes had agreed to drift more quietly. A second passed, then another.

*No footsteps. No voice breaking the air. No rustle of movement.*

*Just the quiet presence and the strange weight of a moment that refused to become anything more.*

*After some time her breathing steadied enough for her to speak. Her voice was shaky and small, and the words came slowly between breaths. She told me that people she trusted had shared explicit photos of her. She told me how it had spread through her friends and how she felt like everything in her life was breaking apart.*

*I stared at the floor as she spoke, watching the fibers of the carpet blur together. I searched for something to say that could help, something that could make even a small part of it better, but nothing came to mind.*

*So instead I wrapped my arms around her.*

*She leaned into the hug and cried into my shoulder, her hands clutching the fabric of my shirt. We stayed there on the hallway floor under the dim lights, holding onto each other while the weight of what she had shared settled quietly around us.*

I never told her this story, but I did approach her the next day and it turned out she was really struggling with school and felt a little lost.

So, I implore you maybe take a moment to analyze your dreams and reach out to those you care about. You never know if your words can save someones' life.

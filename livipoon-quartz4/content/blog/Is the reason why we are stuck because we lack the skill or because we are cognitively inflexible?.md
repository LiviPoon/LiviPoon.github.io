I found it interesting these past couple of days the necessity to be fluid in experiences and places that are quite unfamiliar. In many aspects of my life there have been occasions which I don't know something or I have never encountered a way of thought. My initial reaction usually is to be surprised or taken back, "Why do they think this way?" or "Why is that so wrong?" or even, "Why do I lack those skills?" But is it because I fear the [[The Art of Vulnerability|unknown]] or is it because I do not know [[The Challenge of Being|myself]]?

There are a couple of thoughts. First, why is this wrong? Second, knowing myself better, does it have more to do with the fact that I need to work on my cognitive flexibility?

## Why is this wrong?

There are going to be an infinite amount of times when the situation that presents to you are not going to be what you expect. In many ways this is a good and a bad thing, if the world were predictable then we could do, and be, many different kinds of things in a structured way. However, the world is not predictable. IE: we don't know when we are going to die, we don't know whether or not the choices we make are good ones. And so, to view a particular situation as "wrong" is wrong to begin with. 

## So is it a skill issue or a cognitive inflexibility problem?

It could be both. It probably is true that you lack skills in a particular part of your life which needs tuning and learning from, but it is even more true that you are afraid of the unknown and you don't have the proper support systems to wade into that unknown.

Given these thoughts, what if then, the reason you lack those skills is not the problem, but rather your inability to adjust to the given scenario? Food for thought.

*Though some would argue, why wait to build the proper support system and just throw yourself out there.* This should be taken with a grain of salt, as there could be areas of life where it could do more harm then good, especially if the person hasn't developed the right skills to combat these situations. For example, if the situation is mental one -- suicidal thoughts etc. So if that's the case then maybe figuring out why is more important than simply "fixing the problem"... there are more layers than the eye can see.

# Fun Facts

* Ocean Sunfish are primarily comprised of collagen, which is an extremely energy efficient molecule to produce and make. 

* Ocean Sunfish eat jellyfish which take part in the Diel Vertical Migration (DVM). In order to eat the jellyfish Ocean Sunfish down between 20m to 800m below the surface ([reference](https://web.archive.org/web/20110720094050/http://www.tunalab.unh.edu/molaresearchresults.htm)).

* Ocean Sunfish grow exponentially fast, with one young specimen at the Monterey Bay Aquarium growing from 26 to 399 kg (57 to 880 lbs) and reaching a height of nearly 1.8 m (5 ft 11 in) in 15 months ([reference](https://archive.org/details/fascinationforfi00powe_0/page/270/mode/2up)).

* Females can produce more eggs than any other known vertebrate -- up to 300 million at a time ([reference](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Why-are-there-no-really-big-bony-fishes-A-on-body-Freedman-Noakes/4a62492ee49767aeb1a5933850edc5cf6e806c59)).

There are so many different things to learn in this world and sometimes being smart highlights these gaps. In my eyes this is both blessing and a curse.

I have noticed within my friend groups that these thoughts can develop into patterns that impact our ability to keep healthy mindsets about learning. Some of us develop a crippling anxiety about if we are falling behind or feel intimidated by those who are ahead. 

I spoke to a close mentor recently about how I feel in some ways that math is the limiting factor for me really excelling in my machine learning studies. What he told me was quite eye opening.

The goal shouldn't be to understand these mathematical equations to perfection. 

The goal should be trying to understand the foundations so well that you can intuitively understand the equations.

People who are leaders in their field cannot know everything, but what they can do is use their basic understandings of how certain concepts work together to digest new information easily. Just like a musician can sight read a new piece. A musician learning a new piece cannot perfectly understand the intonations of every measure after one go. But because of their foundational understandings of music they are able to get further in learning the piece from the get-go then if they didn't. It seems like common sense, but it is so important. 

That was enlightening to hear from someone else.

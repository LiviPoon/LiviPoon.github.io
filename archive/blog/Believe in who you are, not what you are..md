title: Learning to Love Who You Are, not What You Are.
date: 2026-03-25

---
By detaching yourself from other's opinions, you allow yourself to relish who you are rather than what you are to others.

I have been spending a lot of my time recently purposefully putting myself in rooms or situations where I haven't before. At first the benefits of doing this were not apparent. I would leave situations a little confused, anxious, or even sad. 

*Did I say the right things at the right time? Did they like me? Do I really want to jumprope, or is it because I want people to view me a certain way because I jumprope?*

However, the more I did it, the more I realized what wasn't for me — and in that process, you quickly catch on to the relationships, places, and things that are worth keeping. Now, if at any moment I realize myself sacrificing my own character, limiting my boundaries, or having an unbalanced level of curiosity to keep something in my own life, I simply remove myself from that situation.

What a beautiful thing it is to touch someone's heart, to be present, to listen to their life stories. But how much more powerful to keep your heart fiercely protected for those you are willing to break it for.

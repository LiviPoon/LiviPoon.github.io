---
title: '"But, I only give people two chances."'
tags:
  - introspection
  - poem
date: 2026-03-10
---
Just wait until you meet someone whose smile<br>
feels like the first light after your long dark,<br>
the kind that finds you after barren years<br>
and lays a hand upon your tired heart.<br>
<br>
Someone who, with the smallest touch, could break<br>
the fragile parts of you you thought were safe,<br>
and with the slightest distance, leave you raw—<br>
and still, you’d pardon them each time they did.<br>
<br>
Someone who pulls forgotten parts of you<br>
up from the depths you never knew were there,<br>
then teaches you to grow beyond your pain,<br>
beyond the self you thought you had to be.<br>
<br>
Someone who still believes in who you are<br>
when all the world has fallen cold and mute,<br>
who cheers for your survival even when<br>
no one else sees the war you’ve had to fight.<br>
<br>
Someone before whom you can come undone<br>
without the weight of shame, without disguise,<br>
and place your darkest secrets in their hands<br>
without the fear they’ll turn them into knives.<br>
<br>
Just wait until you meet someone your heart<br>
has begged itself to leave behind for good,<br>
again, again, again—yet still remains<br>
in every corner of your living mind.<br>
<br>
Someone whose memory does not dissolve,<br>
but lingers softly, sharply, like a ghost—<br>
relentless in its tenderness and ache,<br>
too near to lose, too distant to outrun.<br>
<br>
Someone who, with the barest thought of them,<br>
brings back the butterflies, and with them grief,<br>
as though your heart had never truly learned<br>
the shape of life that did not include them.<br>
<br>
And if you ever found your way back there,<br>
you would not ask them to become the past.<br>
You’d let the older version of them go,<br>
and learn them as they are before you now.<br>
<br>
With open hands and all your tender ache,<br>
you’d learn each scar, each silence, every softness,<br>
and love the person standing in that light,<br>
instead of grieving who they used to be.<br>
<br>
And even if, through countless other lives,<br>
you never got the chance to see them twice,<br>
you still would bless this single life for this:<br>
you once were brave enough to forgive again.<br>
<br>
Because although they may have only been<br>
a passing mercy through your life once more,<br>
it still would have been worth the cost of love<br>
to know them once again, and let them in.<br>
<br>
Just *wait*.
